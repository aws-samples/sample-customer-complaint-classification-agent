Complaint Handling Guidelines

1. Complaint Categories
   - Product/Service Quality Issues
   - Customer Service Experience
   - Technical Problems
   - Billing/Financial Disputes
   - Policy/Procedure Concerns
   - Privacy/Security Issues

2. Priority Levels
   High Priority:
   - Financial loss over $1,000
   - Security breaches
   - Service outages affecting multiple customers
   - Regulatory compliance issues
   - Escalated complaints from regulatory bodies

   Medium Priority:
   - Service disruptions affecting individual customers
   - Billing disputes under $1,000
   - Product functionality issues
   - Unresolved customer service issues
   - Account access problems

   Low Priority:
   - General feedback
   - Minor inconveniences
   - Documentation requests
   - Feature requests
   - Non-urgent clarifications

3. Response Time Requirements
   High Priority: Within 24 hours
   Medium Priority: Within 3 business days
   Low Priority: Within 5 business days

4. Resolution Steps
   a. Acknowledge receipt of complaint
   b. Gather all relevant information
   c. Investigate root cause
   d. Document findings
   e. Propose solution
   f. Implement corrective actions
   g. Follow up with customer
   h. Record outcome

5. Customer Communication Guidelines
   - Be empathetic and professional
   - Acknowledge the customer's concerns
   - Provide clear explanations
   - Set realistic expectations
   - Keep customer informed of progress
   - Document all communications

6. Escalation Path
   Level 1: Customer Service Representative
   Level 2: Team Lead/Supervisor
   Level 3: Department Manager
   Level 4: Executive Team

7. Documentation Requirements
   - Customer details
   - Complaint description
   - Category and priority
   - Investigation findings
   - Actions taken
   - Resolution details
   - Customer feedback
   - Follow-up actions

8. Quality Assurance
   - Regular review of complaint handling
   - Customer satisfaction surveys
   - Response time monitoring
   - Resolution rate tracking
   - Root cause analysis
   - Process improvement recommendations
