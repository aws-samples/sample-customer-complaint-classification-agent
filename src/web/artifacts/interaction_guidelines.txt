Customer Interaction Classification Guidelines

1. Complaints
    - Expressions of dissatisfaction about products, services, or experiences
    - Issues involving potential financial loss or inconvenience
    - Service failures or disruptions
    - Disputes about fees or charges
    - Reports of system issues or errors

2. Feedback
    - Positive comments about products, services, or staff
    - Suggestions for improvements
    - General observations about the banking experience
    - Product or service reviews
    - Constructive criticism without immediate issues

3. Service Requests
- Account maintenance tasks
- Information inquiries
- Product applications
- Technical support needs
- Documentation requests
- Address or personal information updates

4. Urgent Issues
- Security concerns
- Fraud reports
- Immediate financial impact
- System outages affecting multiple customers
- Regulatory or legal threats

Each interaction should be classified into one of these categories and routed to the appropriate team:
- Complaints → Customer Resolution Team
- Feedback → Product & Service Development
- Service Requests → Customer Service Operations
- Urgent Issues → Priority Response Team
